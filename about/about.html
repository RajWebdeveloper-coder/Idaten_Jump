Story

The biggest MTB (Mountain Bike) tournament is known as the “longest and toughest race.” It is not just a test of speed, but a true challenge of a rider’s spirit and skill. For this competition, a special MTB team called “Idaten” is formed.
This story takes place in a world centered around MTB racing, where winning a race is never easy.
One day, while Sho is practicing with his team leader, a mysterious event occurs. A strange MTB frame gate suddenly appears before him. Beyond the gate, powerful MTB riders are waiting.
Sho is transported to an unknown world called the “X-Zone.” In this world, MTB battles are not just races but battles for survival. To win, riders must clear ten extremely difficult stages.
Enemies, allies, victory, and defeat all lead to one question: Will Sho be able to overcome these intense MTB battles and return to his own world?
