About Idaten Jump

Sho Yamato is a young mountain biker who loves speed and challenges. One day, he is suddenly transported to a x zone world. This new world is ruled by intense bike races instead of normal battles. Sho quickly learns that winning races is the only way to survive there. He also discovers that returning home will not be easy. To go back to his house, Sho must become stronger as a rider. He trains hard and improves his biking skills with every race. Along the way, he meets new friends who support him. He also faces powerful rivals who try to stop him. Some enemies use unfair tricks to win races. Sho refuses to give up despite the danger. Each race teaches him an important life lesson. He learns the value of teamwork and trust. Sho slowly collects important victories that move him closer to home. He gains confidence and maturity through his journey. His determination never fades, even when he loses. Every win unlocks new paths and opportunities. Sho believes his family is waiting for him. That belief gives him strength to keep racing. In the end, Sho’s goal is simple: win fairly, grow stronger, and return safely to his home.
